## What changed in the code, beyond the heads

- `tests/test_pendulum.py`: the paragraph "No real bar is perfectly stiff" (book Ch 3, `sec.imc_arrangements`) tells
  one pendulum the step's duration; the tests built a new pendulum for each step length (`pendulum(..., dt)`). Now each
  test builds one pendulum at `dt = 1.0` and steps it by `closed_step(A, duration=tau)`, the book's integrator told
  `tau` (the two agree exactly: checked equal to 0.0 over 200 steps at `tau = 1e-4`). The steel stretch is asserted
  between 1 and 5 micrometers, the book's "a couple of micrometers" (was: under 10); new
  `test_the_rates_of_bar_and_swing` reads the bars' vibrations (440 and 1150 cycles a second, the book's "about a
  thousand times a second") and the slow swing (2.6 s, "about once every two seconds") off the composite's own
  inclination field linearized at the hanging rest; `test_the_bar_sets_the_step` (was `test_the_rod_sets_the_step`)
  adds steps on either side of the limit `2/omega` set by the fastest vibration (4.1e-4 s blows up, 2.1e-4 s holds) and
  the same 1e-3 s step running well on bars of `kappa = 1e4`; the energy check runs over 2 s (was 1 s).
  `test_stiff_rods_approach_rigid_ones` is `test_stiff_bars_approach_rigid_ones`, the book's word.
- `tests/test_copy_pair.py`: the paragraph "Run it at duration 1" (book Ch 3, `ex.copying_learner`) runs the book's
  `copier` of `eqn.copier_wd`, signal from outside and both values sent out (`copying.copy_pair_out`); the tests ran its
  numbers on `copying.copy_pair`, a closed variant with `kappa` as an input and nothing sent out, and checked the book's
  `copier` only by equality with it. Now the numbers run on `copy_pair_out` through `copier_step`, the values read off its
  two outputs; the equality with `copy_pair` is kept. `test_the_exported_copier_sends_both_values_and_steps_as_the_closed_one`
  is `test_the_copier_sends_both_values_as_the_dynamics_functor_does`; "effort field" is "inclination field" and "copy"
  is "copier" in the test names and docstrings, the book's words.
- `tests/test_pushback_copying.py`: new `test_teaching_is_descent_on_the_pushback_mismatch`, the book's claim that the
  added inclination is minus the differential of `(1/2) kappa |lambda_o - pi_c(q, v)|^2`, which no test checked.
- `arrangements/pushback_copying.py`: the head, and local names to the book's (`x` to `v`, `z` to `v_prime`, `e` to
  `teaching`, `gamma, beta` to `load_int, pi_c`); no change of behavior.
