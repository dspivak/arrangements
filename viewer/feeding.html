The page runs one arrangement: a source (the food, the opening, the casing as a mass, gravity, and a dragger that is a spring tied to a memory of where the casing was) wired to a receiver (a battery, a grip, and three stateless parts, the gripper, the opener and the digestor). Each stateless part pushes back on the values it reads; the pushes on one value add, and each part that stores a state moves it by the push it receives. The rates below are those pushes. Written out in the book's terms in background/feeding-imc/feeding-imc.tex.

The model (feeding-model.js) is tested by feeding-model.test.js against the book's background/feeding-imc/check_episode.py: at the starting values shown when the page opens, after 12 units of time the battery is 10.399 and the food 0.074, the grip lets go at 3.058, and the energy lost adds back to the energy at the start. That script is ordinary code; this arrangement is not yet built in the library itself.
